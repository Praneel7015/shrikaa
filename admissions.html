Common questions

Admissions FAQ

Do I need to take an entrance test to join?

No. Admissions at Shrikaa are based on a counselling conversation, not an entrance exam. We assess your current level and goals through discussion.

What if I can’t afford the fees?

Tell us during your counselling session. We offer flexible payment plans and, in exceptional cases, fee concessions for students who demonstrate genuine need. This is part of our mission.

Can I join mid-year?

Yes, subject to seat availability. We will give you a clear picture of what you may have missed and how to catch up. Call us to check current availability.

Does Shrikaa provide study materials?

Yes. Study materials are included in the fee. Students also get access to the AskShrikaa platform with 1,060 MCQs, 1,359 video lessons, and 24 course PDFs.
