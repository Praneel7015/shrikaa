Our Story

About Shrikaa

Founded to eliminate the gap between financial hardship and academic excellence, one student at a time.

Who we are

A movement, not just a coaching institute

At Shrikaa, we are dedicated to transforming the educational landscape by offering affordable, high-quality education to 1st and 2nd PUC, CET, NEET, and JEE students.

Our mission is to make exceptional academic support accessible to students from all financial backgrounds, ensuring that every aspiring learner has the opportunity to excel. By reducing financial barriers, we aim to create a level playing field where talent and hard work are the primary determinants of success.

Shrikaa is not just an educational institution. It is a commitment to social impact. We believe education is a powerful catalyst for change, and our fees reflect that belief.

Our purpose

Mission & Vision

Mission

“To make quality education accessible to every student, regardless of financial background.”

We provide comprehensive curriculum, experienced faculty, and a supportive learning environment designed to empower students to achieve their academic goals.

Vision

“A future where every deserving student from Bengaluru reaches their full academic potential.”

By producing a new generation of informed, capable, and confident individuals, we aspire to drive meaningful change, one student, one family, one community at a time.

What we stand for

Our Values

Affordability

Fees are structured around what students can pay, not what the market will bear. We have never turned away a student because of financial hardship.

Excellence

Affordable never means inferior. Our faculty, materials, and methods are held to the same standard as any premium institution in the city.

Inclusion

Every student, from any background, any medium, any board, receives the same quality of attention and the same belief in their potential.

Results

We measure ourselves by where our students end up, not just how many we enrol. Results are the only proof that matters.

Innovation

From updated study materials to our AskShrikaa AI platform, we continuously invest in better ways for students to learn and practice.

Community

We are present across multiple government PU colleges in Bengaluru, embedded in the communities we serve, not distant from them.
