How to Prepare for KCET from 2nd PUC

A practical plan for Karnataka CET aspirants balancing board exams and entrance prep.

If you are in 2nd PUC Science in Bengaluru, KCET (CET) is often the most realistic path to engineering seats in Karnataka. The mistake most students make is treating boards and CET as two separate worlds.

1. Build PCM foundations first

CET rewards clarity in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics more than memorising shortcuts. Use your PUC syllabus as the spine — then layer CET-style numericals every week.

2. Weekly mock rhythm

One timed sectional test per week beats cramming ten full mocks in the last month. Review every wrong answer the same day.

3. Use AI practice carefully

Tools like AskShrikaa help you ask follow-up questions at night — but keep a notebook of concepts the AI clarified so you can revise offline.

4. When to join coaching

If home practice is inconsistent, structured CET coaching in Bengaluru (with published fees and doubt sessions) can keep you accountable without draining the family budget.
