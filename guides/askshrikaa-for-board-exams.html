Using AskShrikaa Alongside Board Exam Prep

How PUC students can use AskShrikaa for Physics, Chemistry and Biology without replacing teachers.

AskShrikaa is Shrikaa's sister AI learning platform for KCET, NEET and board exam students. It works best as a companion — not a substitute for class.

Ask after class, not instead of class

Use it to clarify a step you missed, generate extra MCQs, or revisit a video explanation the night before a test.

Keep a concept log

Write down the idea in your own words after each AI explanation. That transfer is what sticks in board exams.

Start at askshrikaa.com or join classroom coaching via admissions.
